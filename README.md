# Exposure Time Reasoning (ETR)

Paper: Research Paper No.21 — *Exposure Time and the Structural Trilemma of P versus NP* (January 2026)
DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18289464 · License: CC BY 4.0
Author: Keiichi Shiraishi (AIDE PROJECT) · ORCID 0009-0003-6511-8210

## Summary
Introduces *exposure time*: the minimal temporal depth needed for decisive constraints
to become observable in a search process. The paper proposes a structural framework that
identifies three obstacles any separation proof must address.
It does not claim new lower bounds or a separation of P and NP.
